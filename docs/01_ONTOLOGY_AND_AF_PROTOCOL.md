# Source authority and AF scope

Use the actual source version, current owner instructions and applicable
amendments. The [aod-skills](https://github.com/rpoyau/aod-skills) entry points
separate these roles:

- **AF** governs explicit axioms, declared choices, inference, scope, dependency
  traceability and falsification. Mathematics participates in immediate
  consequences and subsequent constructions; it is not confined to a final tier.
- **AFC** supplies a declared finite calculus. Its identities require their stated
  domain and inputs; they do not establish every AOD construction or physical law.
- **AOD** governs its ontology, notation, manuscript structure and Main/Manual
  ownership under current owner amendments.
- **General Mechanics** supplies compatible presentation support. Its physical
  premises are not inherited merely because its style is used.

AF1–AF7 require axioms once, mathematical derivation, explicit specialization,
no undeclared midstream imports or retrofitting, scoped claims and traceable
falsifiers. RF1–RF5 require distinctions before relations, declared model
constructions, no undeclared absolutes, and no promotion of information,
resolution or representation limits to physical laws. A word match cannot prove
that these requirements were met or violated.

For AOD, Main owns ontology and scoped mathematics, with definitions, hypotheses
and proofs. Manual volumes own implementations, worked calculations, calibration
and applications. Main must stand without Manual premises. Authorized corrections
and additions to Main are possible; ownership does not imply universal immutability.

Preserve existing scientific identity, equations and stable anchors during
terminology repairs. New active names should describe their scientific subject.
Historical compatibility identifiers need explicit provenance, not global renaming.

The linter supplies static diagnostics and source indexes. It does not certify
AF compliance. See [the implemented interfaces](03_COMPILER_LINTER_DESIGN.md)
and [label hygiene](LABEL_HYGIENE.md).
